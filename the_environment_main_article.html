Consumption, Conscientiousness, and the Breaking Point

We live in a world where we have become sanitised and at worse apathetic by all we see and hear...

We are almost 50 years into the economic experiment of neo-liberalism, which sought to allow the individual the freedom to consume as much as they wanted. For over four decades, we have witnessed consumption on a scale never witnessed before.

"Unfettered consumption is in contradiction to a finite world. We have reached the environmental breaking point."

The modern economy works on the basis of all our individual desires being fulfilled, followed by new enticements to propel the ship forward.
