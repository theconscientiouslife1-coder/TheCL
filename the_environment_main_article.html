Δ THE CONSCIENTIOUS LIFE

Consumption and the Breaking Point

The Issue:
We live in a world where we have become sanitised and at worse apathetic by all we see and hear. TV screens and personal devices are constantly churning out the ills of the world, yet unless we experience the catastrophes ourselves, we simply scroll past.

We are almost 50 years into the economic experiment of neo-liberalism. For over four decades, we have witnessed consumption on a scale never witnessed before. This has led to a situation where individuals are unaware of the effects their consumption has on the world.

"Unfettered consumption is in contradiction to a finite world. We have reached the environmental breaking point."

The modern economy works on the basis of all our individual desires being fulfilled. As Schopenhauer observed, once we have attained a desire, we move on to the next. The environment pays the heavy price—from raw materials to manufacturing and disposal.
