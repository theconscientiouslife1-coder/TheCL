Consumption, Conscientiousness, and the Breaking Point

We live in a world where we have become sanitised and at worse apathetic by all we see and hear...

We are almost 50 years into the economic experiment of neo-liberalism, which sought to allow the individual the freedom to consume as much as they wanted...

"Unfettered consumption is in contradiction to a finite world. We have reached the environmental breaking point."

Yet, there is a debt to be paid for these desires. It is the environment that pays the heavy price—from raw materials to manufacturing and disposal.
