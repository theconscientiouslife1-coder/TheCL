Environment

Consumption, Conscientiousness, and the Breaking Point

Exploring the intersection of our daily consumption habits and the ecological health of our planet.

The environment is often seen as something "out there"—a distant forest or a melting glacier. However, the environment is right here, in every plastic bag, every hamburger, and every kilowatt of electricity we use. Our conscientious choices are the only way forward.

Small Steps, Massive Impact

Reducing waste isn't just about recycling; it's about rethinking what we buy in the first place. By adopting a mindset of intentionality, we can reduce our footprint significantly without sacrificing quality of life.
