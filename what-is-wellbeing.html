What is Well-Being?

In today’s world as people seek peace of mind from all the distractions and demands that this world puts on us. We hear the term well-being banded about. But what is Well-Being and what is its importance in achieving the Conscientious Life? This pillar of the blog builds on the what was in the DIET & FITNESS columns. The work/effort that one puts into their diet and fitness is the foundation to be able to have a level of Well-being that allows us to elevate our sense of self.

What is it?

If we take the word being; this refers to existence and when we put it in the context of a human it refers to how we are existing. Obviously, all things exist, but from our perspective it is multifaceted; we have various ways in which we live i.e. work, family, leisure etc. Adding the term “Well” is that our existence has both a sense of balance and that we generally feel a level of satisfaction. But it also entails that we are existing and are at one with our nature.

The World Health Organisation defines it as a positive state experienced by individuals and societies; it is understood as a positive, holistic state (including mental and spiritual) that enables individuals and communities to thrive. It is determined by social, economic and environmental conditions and as the Conscientious Life blog will strongly emphasise how critical the environment is to our Well-being.

Well-Being is not a term that has just appeared over the past few decades, it is has pre-occupied humans over the millennia. Aristotle determined that to live well there needed to be a proper appreciation of the way in which such goods as friendship, pleasure, virtue, honour and wealth fit together as a whole. This he labelled eudaimonia, which refers to human flourishing. For Aristotle, this can only be achieved through action. It is not a feeling of positivity which reinforces both or feelings and our rationale to the way we view those elements, which leads to a virtuous circle. It acts like a self-reinforcing, positive feedback loop where each success fuels the next. As Aristotle reportedly once said: “We are what we repeatedly do. Excellence, therefore, is not an act but a habit.”

Some consider Well-Being as exclusively associated with happiness, we do not argue that it has a huge baring on how we feel and view the world but happiness is a fleeting emotion, contentment is rather the goal; a feeling that perseveres, which implies that someone is at one with the various elements of oneself, living in the NOW and not dwelling in our minds of what we don’t have. When we have a strong sense of Well-Being, we understand that yes, maybe today is not the best day one is having, but has the knowledge & experience that such a day will end and the next day will bring new possibilities. Like the sun that rises in the morning, one is filled with hope that today will be a good day.
