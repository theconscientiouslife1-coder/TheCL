What is Well-Being?

In today’s world as people seek peace of mind from all the distractions and demands that this world puts on us, we hear the term well-being banded about[cite: 98, 99]. This pillar of the blog builds on what was in the Diet & Fitness columns[cite: 100]. The work/effort that one puts into their diet and fitness is the foundation to be able to have a level of Well-being that allows us to elevate our sense of self[cite: 101].

Adding the term “Well” to our existence means that it has both a sense of balance and that we generally feel a level of satisfaction[cite: 105]. The World Health Organisation defines it as a positive state experienced by individuals and societies; it is understood as a positive, holistic state (including mental and spiritual) that enables individuals and communities to thrive[cite: 107, 108].

One of those who thought deeply about this was Aristotle who determined that to live well there needed to be a proper appreciation of the way in which such goods as friendship, pleasure, virtue, honour and wealth fit together as a whole[cite: 112]. This he labelled eudaimonia, which refers to human flourishing[cite: 113]. For Aristotle, this can only be achieved through action[cite: 115]. Excellence, therefore, is not an act but a habit[cite: 127].

When we have a strong sense of Well-Being, we understand that yes, maybe today is not the best day one is having, but we have the knowledge & experience that such a day will end and the next day will bring new possibilities[cite: 131]. We live in the NOW and do not dwell in our minds of what we don’t have[cite: 130].
