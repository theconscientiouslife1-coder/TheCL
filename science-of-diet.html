The Science of Our Diet

****This blog will only repeat scientific information that has been cited by creditable institutions.****

A conscientious life is built upon a foundation of awareness—not just of our actions and thoughts, but of the very fuel that sustains them. Understanding the science of diet isn't just about nutrition; it's about understanding the biological machinery that drives our potential.

The Gut-Brain Axis

One of the most profound scientific discoveries in recent years is the depth of the connection between our digestive system and our brain, often referred to as the enteric nervous system (ENS).

When the Gut-Brain Axis is in-sync we are more balanced and are in a higher state allowing us to take advantage of the many positives which come from being clear in body and mind.
