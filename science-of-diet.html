The Science of Our Diet

This blog will only repeat scientific information that has been cited by creditable institutions.

The science of food has advanced past just the understanding of the nutrients that can be attained from foods but we have a clearer picture of what food compositions have on the body both physically and psychologically. However, there is significant information which is very much in the pseudoscience bucket. This leads to misinformation which subsequently contradicts what is and isn’t good for someone’s WELL-BEING.

Research is gaining a deeper understanding of how our gut impacts us. When the gut-brain axis is in-sync we are more balanced and are in a higher state allowing us to take advantage of the many positives which come from being clear in body and mind.

The Gut-Brain Axis

The gut and the brain are in constant communication via the vagus nerve. This connection influences digestion, mood, immunity, and stress responses. A healthy microbiome—supported by fermented foods, prebiotics, and fibre—is crucial for maintaining this axis.
