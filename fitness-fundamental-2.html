Fitness | Fundamental Number 2

This blog makes reference to 3 fundamental elements that are the foundation for the potential one has in life[cite: 2]. The second fundamental is fitness; a key foundational stone driving new ideas/goals and the focus to achieve those aspirations[cite: 3].

While many focus on moderate activity like 10,000 steps, we focus on the benefits of regular intense exercise[cite: 5, 6]. Pushing your cardiovascular system improves heart and lung health, blood pressure, and aerobic capacity (VO2 max) more effectively than moderate activity alone[cite: 10]. This allows more blood to be circulated around the physical body, benefiting both our physical and psychological states[cite: 11, 12].

A key biological benefit is the increase of the protein brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF)[cite: 14]. This protein is essential for developing new NEURONS (neurogenesis), which is vital for maintaining cognitive flexibility and forming new memories[cite: 15, 16]. This process, occurring in the hippocampus, explains the improved cognition and emotional state that accompanies exercise[cite: 17].

Consider the blood flow: 15% of the body’s blood supply is received by the brain, even though its mass is only 3%[cite: 18]. Through neurovascular coupling (NVC), exercise ensures an adequate supply of oxygen and glucose to active brain regions, sustaining synaptic plasticity—the cellular basis for learning and memory[cite: 19, 21].

To summarise, this category along with DIET and REST are the “3-Fs” indispensable to ensuring the correct foundation is in place to accomplish one’s dreams[cite: 24].
