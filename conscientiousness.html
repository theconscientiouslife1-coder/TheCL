Conscientiousness

The core trait of intentional living: moving beyond rash behavior toward careful, reasoned action.

Conscientiousness is the foundation of our philosophy. It is the ability to lift the veil of a self-interested nature and begin to see the world as it really is, acting in accordance with reason and duty.

"Our freedom is predicated on the fact we have reason, which distinguishes us and allows us to act autonomously."
