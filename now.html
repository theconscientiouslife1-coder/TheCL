Now - What's the Rush

The Earth orbits the sun at a speed of 100,000KM per hour. For which the sun is moving around our galaxy at over 800,000Km per hour. Objectively speaking we are hurtling through space with the astronomical end goal of completing a cycle of each orbit and like the many analogies before, we are just moments in what seems an infinite line.

Why you may ask are we telling you this fact? One reason is to highlight that there is no one true meaning to what this world is about. What we can discern from our experience is that the world is in motion and change. We see it in the day-to-day activities of society, to the heavenly motions above. And our BEING is no different, we are constantly in motion and this invariably brings change. What this way of being for us entails is a continual thinking of the next thing we need to do, attain, which requires us to consider the many steps/activities in that pursuit.

All our STRIVING, wanting, desiring has us in a forever tailspin, relentlessly chasing the next want. Arthur Schopenhauer was one of the first Western philosophers to understand that life is dictated by this need to strive. He terms the mechanism the Will; “its desires are unlimited, its claims inexhaustible, and every satisfied desire gives birth to a new one. No possible satisfaction in the world could suffice to still its craving, set a final goal to its demands, and fill the bottomless pit of its heart”.

Thus you may ask what is all our striving, planning, executing leading to exactly? Of course, we want to ensure we have the basics in life for ourselves and our loved ones, however in this post-modern world where life is in many ways reduced to a synthetic endeavour i.e. commercialism, what Eastern philosophy refers to The Veil of Maya; the deeper meaning of life is hidden or obstructed from us, therefore we fail to see the bigger picture. We summersault from one thing to the next, not giving any time to reflect on the now. We physically live in the now but psychologically our minds wander between the three-time phases i.e. past present and future. Being pulled by the many facets of our lives and nature the now becomes subsumed within the other time phases. The now is forever but being conscious of it is negligible.

How often do we pause in our day to look what is occurring around us, to experience a world that has no bearing on our individual needs/wants but to get a semblance of the HERE/NOW AXIS? As our lives become ever more frantic, we hear of pop-cultural methods to try momentarily to detach from this striving. One such example is mindfulness, an exercise which tries to get us to focus in the space/time we find ourselves at a given moment. But as noted this does not provided the sanctum, we yearn for to dispel us from this ever wanting, needing.

And with all this motion comes stress. This constant struggle to meet/fulfil the never ending wants that this world projects upon us. We are bombarded by a never-ending cycle of wants. No sooner has summer ended the focus shifts to the Christmas period. What is the next thing to want. The problem this time with unfettered desires, that consist mainly in the guise of consumption, is that the level of our consumption is beyond what the earth can provide. On an annual basis, the ecological footprint of human consumption is greater than the amount of resources that can be regenerated within a year.

What’s the alternative you may ask? It is the Conscientious Life. For starters we are the only conscious beings who can reflect and communicate what we experience. We need to stop and just take in all we experience with our senses. Our life is but a journey of birth to death with our constants stress to fulfil one thing after another makes no difference to our end destination. The sooner we grasp that fact the better.
