NOW – What’s the rush?

The Earth orbits the sun at 100,000KM per hour. We are hurtling through space, and our BEING is no different; we are constantly in motion. This brings a continual thinking of the next thing we need to do or attain.

All our STRIVING has us in a forever tailspin. Arthur Schopenhauer understood that life is dictated by the "Will"—unlimited desires where every satisfied desire gives birth to a new one. This bottomless pit of craving prevents us from experiencing the here/now axis.

What’s the alternative? It is the Conscientious Life. We need to stop and take in all we experience with our senses. Our life is but a journey from birth to death; constant stress to fulfill one want after another makes no difference to our destination. The sooner we grasp that, the better.
