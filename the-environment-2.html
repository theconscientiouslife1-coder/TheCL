The Environment II

Following on from the initial article, attempting to live the Conscientious Life will have consequences for the current way of living. The false FREEDOM we believe in will be sacrificed. We live in a finite world and eventually endless striving will meet its end.

The Conscientious Life is at its essence, autonomous. In this environmental emergency, the Kantian concept of autonomy is extremely apt; acting in accordance with one's moral duty rather than one's desires.

What we attain or are given makes us not owners but rather TRUSTEES. We should always leave the world in a better place than we found it for the next generation of gatekeepers. It is very much a case of lifting the veil.
