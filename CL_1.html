The Conscientious Life (Part 1)

What does it mean to be Conscientious? It is one of the Big Five personality traits, defined as showing or doing something with careful attention. The opposite would be behaving rashly, with no consideration for the consequences of such behaviour.

Our freedom is predicated on the fact we have reason, which allows us to consciously consider what is in front of us and act autonomously. Immanual Kant spoke of a Kingdom of Ends, which implied that one acts toward others and the environment as an end in themselves, not just a means to a subjective end.

The first step is lifting the veil and beginning to see the world as it really is. By being diligent and thoughtful, we move away from atomised existences and toward a life of duty and reasoned action.
