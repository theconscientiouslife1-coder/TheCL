Diet Next Steps

If you have read THE SCIENCE OF OUR DIET, you will understand that reaching your optimal level requires a strong focus on ensuring you are ingesting the ideal foods that will contribute to you having not only a sound physiology but also have your mind in a clear frame. We are what we eat; if we ingest rubbish then we will undoubtedly have a physical state that will not only be inferior but will lead to our psychological state being unclear at best. If we are eating the food types that not only provide the ultimate nourishment we need but ensures that the gut-brain axis is aligned then we are building the bedrock on which we will be able to facilitate the Conscientious Life.

Research has shown that the ideal diet is one that leans heavily on a plant forward, whole food diet; emphasizes plant foods—fruits, vegetables, whole grains, legumes, nuts, and seeds—as the foundation of meals, while reducing or sparingly consuming animal products and refined ingredients.[1]

This blog is not advocating an exclusive vegan diet, but it does understand and promotes a diet that attempts to ingest up to ⅔ of one’s weekly meals being of a plant-forward basis. There is no cold turkey scenario being suggested. It is fundamental that the change is in an incremental manner. We are well aware of the various diet trends that prescribe to achieving rapid weight loss or gain muscle in no time. The Conscientious Life advocates three meals a day. It would be prudent to look to the first meal of the day where changes can be made i.e. breakfast.

We are generally fasting for on average eight hours per day. Therefore, it would be hugely beneficial to provide your body with the best nourishment after such a period. Fruit and wholegrains i.e. porridge should be the focus. Slow release that will satisfy through one’s busy mornings. No one is advocating for one’s food consumption to be bland or to be focused entirely on the nourishment to be attained, however the importance in dieting on fruits, vegetables, whole grains, legumes, nuts, and seeds cannot be over emphasised. To have a solid building the foundation must be sound. The meals for lunch and dinner require more thought than breakfast but you are only having to think outside the box for seven meals. Yet variety is important and more time is required in preparation. Lunches should be in the variety of salads as the majority of the food types can be easily included. Dinners like stews can be cooked in big batches and allows one to then consume over a number of evenings.

Remember that though there is time required, when one is preparing such meals, especially dinners, one is not only destressing from the day but being in the moment, focusing on the NOW taking oneself away from the constant striving and what is next thing to desire. Planning is very important and if you have the different plans for a week’s diet then variety can be introduced. The Conscientious Life cannot advocate the importance of a wholesome diet that includes the key healthy food types. Giving the time at the outset to plan, will make the change less intrusive from your current diet as you transition to such meals. The benefits outweigh the somewhat sacrifice one will achieve when both removing those food types that are detrimental to one’s health and time given to preparing such meals.
