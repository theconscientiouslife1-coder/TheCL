Diet Next Steps

If you have read THE SCIENCE OF OUR DIET, you will understand that reaching your optimal level requires a strong focus on ensuring you are ingesting the ideal foods that will contribute to you having not only a sound physiology but also have your mind in a clear frame[cite: 125]. We are what we eat; if we ingest rubbish then we will undoubtedly have an inferior physical state and an unclear psychological state[cite: 126]. By eating food types that provide ultimate nourishment and ensure the gut-brain axis is aligned, we build the bedrock for the Conscientious Life[cite: 127].

Research has shown that the ideal diet leans heavily on a plant forward, whole food diet; emphasizing fruits, vegetables, whole grains, legumes, nuts, and seeds while reducing animal products and refined ingredients[cite: 128, 129]. This blog promotes a diet that attempts to ingest up to ⅔ of one’s weekly meals on a plant-forward basis[cite: 130]. This is not a "cold turkey" scenario; the change should be incremental[cite: 131].

The Conscientious Life advocates three meals a day[cite: 133]. It would be prudent to start changes with breakfast[cite: 133]. Since we fast for an average of eight hours, providing the best nourishment afterward is hugely beneficial[cite: 134, 135]. Fruit and wholegrains like porridge provide slow-release energy for busy mornings[cite: 136]. Variety is important, and lunches should include salads where many food types can be easily included[cite: 140, 141]. Dinners like stews can be cooked in big batches to be consumed over several evenings[cite: 142].

When preparing these meals, especially dinners, you are not only destressing but focusing on the NOW, taking yourself away from constant striving[cite: 143]. Planning is very important; giving time at the outset will make the transition less intrusive and the benefits far outweigh the sacrifice[cite: 144, 146, 147].
