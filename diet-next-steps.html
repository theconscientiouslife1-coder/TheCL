Diet Next Steps

If you have read THE SCIENCE OF OUR DIET, you will understand that reaching your optimal level requires a strong focus on ensuring you are ingesting the ideal foods. If we are eating food types that ensure the gut-brain axis is aligned, then we are building the bedrock on which we will be able to facilitate the Conscientious Life.

Research has shown that the ideal diet is one that leans heavily on a plant forward, whole food diet. This blog understands and promotes a diet that attempts to ingest up to ⅔ of one’s weekly meals being of a plant-forward basis.

Remember that though there is time required, when one is preparing such meals, especially dinners, one is not only destressing from the day but being in the moment, focusing on the NOW taking oneself away from the constant striving.
