Belief

Belief is a cornerstone of the Conscientious Life. It is an acceptance that something is true. A psychological theory introduced in the 20th century was self-efficacy theory; where people who seek to progress through difficult challenges are considered to have high self-efficacy.

If the chaos of the world is not impacting you directly, you must separate what you are attempting from what you have no control over. It is at its darkest hour that we require belief more than ever. Without it, we will be but hands searching in the dark, and the endeavour will be doomed. While some believe abilities are static, this blog advocates that our abilities can grow and develop through effort and learning.

The journey of one thousand miles begins with a first step, but that step can only arise out of a belief that the journey will be successful. The 3 FUNDAMENTALS (Diet, Exercise, Rest) are prerequisites, and once the whole is in tune, belief in oneself will course through one’s being. Keep fighting, keep believing!
