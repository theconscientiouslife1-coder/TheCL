Δ The Conscientious Life Back to Well-Being

Belief

Belief is a cornerstone of the Conscientious Life. It is the belief that when one puts one’s mind to something, anything is possible[cite: 52, 53]. A psychological theory introduced in the 20th century was self-efficacy theory; where people who seek to progress through difficult challenges are considered to have high self-efficacy[cite: 59, 60].

Belief is the catalyst for all our actions[cite: 64]. Without it, we will be but hands searching in the dark, and the endeavour will be doomed[cite: 65]. While some believe abilities are static, this blog advocates that our abilities can grow and develop through effort and learning[cite: 72]. This virtuous cycle allows belief to take hold, making us consider that anything is possible[cite: 76].

Nevertheless, groundwork needs to be done. The 3 FUNDAMENTALS (Diet, Exercise, Rest) are prerequisites, and once the whole is in tune, belief in oneself will course through one’s being[cite: 77]. Keep fighting, keep believing! [cite: 78]
