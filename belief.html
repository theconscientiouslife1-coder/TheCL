Belief

This article is an attempt to highlight the importance of belief in what we are aiming to be. Whether it be just the first three pillars i.e. Diet, Fitness & Well-being or leading to a Conscientious Life, belief is another cornerstone. Belief that when one put one’s mind to something anything is possible. It may seem like a hollow suggestion to try and stay positive in yourself and what you are trying to achieve, especially if we are trying to look beyond our own personal needs, when we are witnessing an extremely turbulent time in the world, where all seems chaotic.

However, one thing to remember, when all seems lost, if the chaos is not impacting you directly, then you have to separate what you are attempting from what you have no control whatsoever over. It is at its darkest hour that we require belief more than ever. It’s when things seem at their worst you must not quit.

Belief is defined as; an acceptance that something is true. A psychological theory introduced in the 20th century was self-efficacy theory; where people who seek to progress through difficult challenges these are considered to have high self-efficacy. When we set out to achieve an objective, one that will require effort and perhaps sacrifices, if we do not believe we can accomplish the goal, we will in the majority of cases not succeed. But even before that we will unlikely even dare attempt such endeavours.

As with any significant venture, it will be hard and testing at the best of times. But an atomised individual, whose only goal is to meet their own desires, will unlikely have the necessary belief to carry out anything that goes beyond their immediate needs. In our youth, we dream of the endless possibilities that one can achieve in this world. But as time moves by, we fall into the trap of a life that just ebbs and flows with the daily grind. That does not have to be the case.

Throughout the Conscientious Life blog, reference is made to the virtuous cycle where actions lead to a further propulsion of our abilities. Some believe that our abilities are static and unchanging, whereas others, including this blog, believe that our abilities can grow and develop through effort and learning. Which re-enforces our belief in who we are and what we want to be and achieve.

Cliches are unfortunately the antidote to LIFE. The journey of one thousand miles begins with a first step. But that step can only arise out of a belief that the journey will be successful. As there are vicious circles, which further hasten our disbelief in ourselves, there is, as mentioned, a virtuous cycle which allows belief to take hold. Groundwork needs to be done. The 3 FUNDAMENTALS (Diet, Exercise, Rest) are prerequisites and once the whole is in tune and balanced the belief in one’s self will course through one’s being. Keep fighting, keep believing!
