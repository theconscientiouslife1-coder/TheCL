Pillar 1

Diet | Fundamental Number 1

What we ingest determines multiple facets of our being. From our physicality, to our psychological state, to our sleep pattern to our overall sense of ourselves. One of the most important things we can do to assist our WELL-BEING is to ensure we are living in a balanced manner.

Our diet is key to attaining that balance. In this category we will discuss the science of food and the impact it has on us. We are now induced to believe we must be on 24/7 and because of this we give little time to the basics of ourselves, diet being one of those and willing to sacrifice the necessities of ourselves to fleeting wants/desires. “Have food on the go to ensure you can keep going” to where, one might ask!

This blog does not in any way suggest that one’s diet must resemble that of a monk but it does want to convey in the strongest terms that the majority of one’s diet must be made up of vegetables, pulses nuts & fruit, this has been a cornerstone of food science for almost 50 years; THE FOOD PYRAMID. The focus of the foods are those mentioned and to reduce or restrict synthetic sugars and trans/saturated fats.

As the old adage goes “we are what we eat” which implies that if we want our physiology/psychology to be at a healthy level, the food we consume must be natural and not processed (or at least to a minimum) and if we do then we are consuming what the body really needs. What is written is very much from a COMMON SENSE perspective, as food science continues to evolve, the importance of a natural diet shows manifold benefits, which even if one has suspicions of what science informs us, we have seen over the past 50 years an increase in life expectancy from 70 years to almost 80 years for both sexes.

There are multiple variables as to why life expectancy has increased but a fundamental is a person’s diet. We are all well acquainted with the food pyramid but this is an invaluable resource to understand the importance of having a well-balanced diet and attempting to reach a level of ingesting optimum food sources. What is written here is nothing new to you, but what is worth noting is the importance of it to who we are and want to be with regard to the Conscientious Life.

This pillar of the blog, will provide methods to gain habits that ensure that one is only ingesting what is the best for us. Also, we will provide both statistics and scientific insights that highlights the benefits not only from a physiological perspective but how it impacts us psychologically. In addition, this area will loop back to how important Diet is to all the other categories and most importantly to us.
