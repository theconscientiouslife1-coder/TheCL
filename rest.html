Rest | Fundamental Number 3

The third and what seems like the easiest of the “3-Fs” is rest[cite: 81]. Rest is aligned with the WELL-BEING pillar but is crucial in establishing and nurturing the other two fundamentals i.e. Diet & Fitness[cite: 82].

There is a virtuous circle at play between the "3-Fs"[cite: 83]. If we are eating well, we give our body the necessary ingredients to optimize ourselves during exercise; in turn, exerting the body assists our rest[cite: 83, 84]. When we speak of rest we are referring specifically to sleep[cite: 85]. If we do not experience enough sleep, there are severe consequences if this becomes a prolonged state[cite: 85, 86].

In attempting to have a Conscientious Life, we need to be able to detach and switch off from our everyday[cite: 90]. All that matters is being very conscious of the NOW[cite: 91]. Mindlessly scrolling on computers is not rest; it is disengaging from ourselves[cite: 93, 94, 95]. Instead, rest must be worked on incrementally, such as a walk in a natural setting[cite: 95]. Ultimately, MOTIVATION is vital to achieving suitable rest[cite: 96].
