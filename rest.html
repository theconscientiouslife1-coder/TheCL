The Conscientious Life Back to Well-Being

Rest | Fundamental Number 3

The third and what seems like the easiest of the “3-FS” is rest. Rest is aligned with the WELL-BEING pillar but is crucial in establishing and nurturing the other two fundamentals i.e. DIET & FITNESS.

There is a virtuous circle at play between the "3-Fs". If we are eating well, then we are giving our body the necessary ingredients to allow us to optimise the body when we exercise. When we are eating well and exerting our body, rest will both assist the other two elements but the other two will assist us in our rest. When we speak of rest we are referring specifically to sleep, which as we know if we do not experience enough sleep there are severe consequences if this becomes a prolonged state.

Again, we are well versed, both from our own experience and the countless information we hear on the importance of sleep to our well-being and the negative ramifications if we are sleep deprived. No one person is the same, and sleep is no different. Some can thrive on only five to six hours. Others need eight and in other cases others need nine plus hours. What is key is to have a deep sleep.

In attempting to have a Conscientious Life, we need to be able to detach and switch off from our everyday. All that matters is being very conscious of the NOW. We are now programmed to be "on" all the time. What one thinks of sitting down and scrolling/swiping endlessly on our computers, can at times see us waste literally hours of our day. Where that time could have been spent on something that really takes you away from the daily chores, we are instead mindlessly disengaging with ourselves. This is not rest, quite the opposite.

But like all things this is not something that can be achieved in just stating “I am now stopping and thinking of only what I am doing at this point in time/space.” Rather this, like the other two elements, needs to be worked on incrementally. As an example, instead of jumping on the sofa after a long day, go for a walk and try to have some moment in a natural setting i.e. a park even for 30 minutes. This does not take much to do but like previously discussed MOTIVATION is vital to achieving suitable rest.
