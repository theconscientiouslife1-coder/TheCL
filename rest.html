Rest | Fundamental Number 3

The third and what seems like the easiest of the “3-Fs” is rest. Rest is aligned with the WELL-BEING pillar but is crucial in establishing and nurturing the other two fundamentals i.e. DIET & FITNESS.

There is a virtuous circle at play between the "3-Fs". If we are eating well, we give our body the necessary ingredients to optimize ourselves during exercise; in turn, exerting the body assists our rest. When we speak of rest we are referring specifically to sleep. If we do not experience enough sleep, there are severe consequences if this becomes a prolonged state.

No one person is the same, and sleep is no different. Some thrive on five to six hours, others need nine plus. What is key is to have a deep sleep.

In attempting to have a Conscientious Life, we need to be able to detach and switch off from our everyday. All that matters is being very conscious of the NOW. Mindlessly scrolling on computers is not rest; it is disengaging from ourselves. This needs to be worked on incrementally, such as a walk in a natural setting. Ultimately, MOTIVATION is vital to achieving suitable rest.
