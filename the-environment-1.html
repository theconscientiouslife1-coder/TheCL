The Environment I

We live in a world where we have become sanitised and at worse apathetic by all we see and hear[cite: 2]. TV screens, personal devices are constantly churning out the ills of the world, nevertheless, if we are not experiencing the catastrophises for ourselves, we simply scroll past and tune out[cite: 3].

The environment crisis is no different... the world that will remain will be nothing to what any civilisation has encountered[cite: 4, 6]. How did we get here? Well one key component is the economic experiment, which began 50 years ago, neo-liberalism[cite: 7].

Unfettered consumption is a total contradiction in a finite world[cite: 14]. For over four decades we have seen consumption on a scale never witnessed before[cite: 15]. From figure 1. below, energy consumption has more than doubled in that period[cite: 16].
