The Environment

We live in a world where we have become sanitised and at worse apathetic by all we see and hear. TV screens, personal devices are constantly churning out the ills of the world, nevertheless, if we are not experiencing the catastrophises for ourselves, we simply scroll past and tune out.

The space where we experiment with the ideas we have within, engage with others, work, enjoy, rest is now in a slow decline. How did we get here? One key component is the economic experiment, which began 50 years ago, neo-liberalism; it denotes a conception of freedom as an overarching social value associated with reducing state functions.

The Conscientious Life requires you to make informed decisions and think beyond the immediate. We must understand where a product came from and if it can be recycled. An old adage was REDUCE, REUSE, RECYCLE in that order. By making informed autonomous decisions, we re-enforce our BEING.
