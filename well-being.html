What is Well-Being?

In today’s world, as people seek peace of mind from all the distractions and demands that this world puts on us, we hear the term well-being banded about. But what is Well-Being and what is its importance in achieving the Conscientious Life? This pillar builds on what was in the DIET and FITNESS columns. The work/effort that one puts into their diet and fitness is the foundation to be able to have a level of Well-being that allows us to elevate our sense of self.

If we take the word being; this refers to existence and when we put it in the context of a human it refers to how we are existing. Adding the term “Well” implies that our existence has both a sense of balance and that we generally feel a level of satisfaction. The World Health Organisation defines it as a positive state experienced by individuals and societies; it is understood as a positive, holistic state (including mental and spiritual) that enables individuals and communities to thrive. It is determined by social, economic and environmental conditions.

Aristotle determined that to live well there needed to be a proper appreciation of the way in which such goods as friendship, pleasure, virtue, honour and wealth fit together as a whole. This he labelled eudaimonia, which refers to human flourishing. For Aristotle, this can only be achieved through action. Excellence, therefore, is not an act but a habit. When all those factors are in sync, the sense of meaning in what we do allows us to thrive.

Some consider Well-Being as exclusively associated with happiness; however, happiness is a fleeting emotion. Contentment is rather the goal—a feeling that perseveres, which implies that someone is at one with the various elements of oneself, living in the NOW and not dwelling in our minds on what we don’t have.
